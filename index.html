What is sustainable farming?

Sustainable Farming is a way of farming that aims to protect the environment, conserve natural resources, and use available resources efficiently. It also considers the long-term needs of farmers, communities, and food production. Sustainable agriculture seeks to maintain environmental quality, support the economic viability of farms, and improve the quality of life of farmers and society.

Land Agriculture

System of Rice Intensification (SRI)

The System of Rice Intensification (SRI) is a rice-growing approach that improves the management of plants, soil, water, and nutrients. It aims to produce healthy rice plants while using resources such as water and seeds more efficiently.

Land Agriculture

Integrated Rice-Duck Farming System (IRDFS)

The Integrated Rice-Duck Farming System combines rice cultivation with duck raising in the same field. Ducks can help manage some weeds and pests while providing additional products such as eggs and meat, giving farmers another source of income.

Mangrove-Friendly Aquasilviculture

Mangrove-Friendly Aquasilviculture combines aquaculture with mangrove conservation. It allows aquatic species to be raised while maintaining mangrove vegetation, helping support both coastal livelihoods and healthy ecosystems.

Water Efficiency

Direct Wet-Seeding

Direct Wet-Seeding is a rice-growing method where pre-germinated seeds are planted directly into a wet, well-prepared field. It can reduce labor and establishment costs while helping farmers use water more efficiently.

Aquaculture

Seaweed Farming

Seaweed farming is the cultivation of seaweeds in coastal waters for food, carrageenan, and other useful products. It provides livelihood opportunities for coastal communities and can support sustainable marine production when properly managed.

Back to Types of Farming
Aquaculture

Integrated Multi-Trophic Aquaculture (IMTA)

Integrated Multi-Trophic Aquaculture (IMTA) combines different aquatic species in one farming system. Nutrients and waste from one species can be used by other organisms, helping improve resource use and promote more sustainable aquaculture.
