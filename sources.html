Sources & Research

Official agencies, research institutes, and studies referenced for this project:

  • Bureau of Fisheries and Aquatic Resources (BFAR)
    Guidelines on mangrove-friendly aquasilviculture and sustainable coastal fisheries.
  • Philippine Rice Research Institute (PhilRice)
    Research on System of Rice Intensification (SRI), Direct Wet-Seeding, and Rice-Duck Farming.
  • Southeast Asian Fisheries Development Center (SEAFDEC)
    Studies on Integrated Multi-Trophic Aquaculture (IMTA) and seaweed farming in Southeast Asia.
